At a glance

Procolor Film Academy in short

Procolor Film Academy (PFA) is a training academy in Sector 35C, Chandigarh, where students learn photography, cinematography, video editing and smartphone content creation through hands-on, practical courses. Students rate it 4.9 on Google, and this page reproduces those reviews word for word.

What is Procolor Film Academy?

Procolor Film Academy (PFA) is a training academy in Sector 35C, Chandigarh, where students learn photography, cinematography, video editing and smartphone content creation through hands-on, practical courses. Students rate it 4.9 on Google, and this page reproduces those reviews word for word.

Where can I learn photography in Chandigarh?

Procolor Film Academy teaches a 2-month Diploma in Photography in Sector 35C, Chandigarh. 109 of the 164 written reviews on this page mention photography, and the academy is rated 4.9 on Google. Student Naman Aggarwal wrote: “I got to learn so many things in photography at procolor. Gagan sir is an excellent teacher and the staff is so helpful.”

Where can I learn cinematography in Chandigarh?

Procolor Film Academy teaches a 2-month Diploma in Cinematography in Sector 35C, Chandigarh, and 60 written reviews mention cinematography. Student saurav nivaan wrote: “i did 2 months course from this academy and i have learnt many things about cinematography”

Procolor Film Academy teaches Master Video Editing, a 1-month course, in Sector 35C, Chandigarh. A recent reviewer, Ansh Film, wrote: “my son learned video editing in one month.” Another, Priyanshu, wrote: “The teaching style is easy to understand, and the overall learning experience was amazing.”

Where can I learn content creation in Chandigarh?

Procolor Film Academy runs Creator Lab (From Phone to Feed in 3 Weeks), a 3-week content creation course in Sector 35C, Chandigarh, covering mobile shooting, smartphone editing and gimbal movements.
